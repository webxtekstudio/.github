# Webxtek Studio

Web studio based in Leiria, Portugal. Websites, web apps, AI systems, branding and the
infrastructure behind them, since 2012.

## What we do

**Websites and portals**
Corporate websites, landing pages, multilingual platforms and online stores at any scale.
Fast, accessible (WCAG/ARIA), GDPR compliant and built for technical SEO.

**Branding and design**
Brand identity, guidelines, UX/UI design, interactive prototypes and design systems.

**Web apps and SaaS**
SaaS platforms, client portals, dashboards, backoffices, marketplaces, and ERP and CRM
integrations.

**AI and automation**
AI chatbots and agents, company knowledge bases (RAG), voice agents, semantic search and
automated workflows.

**Video and campaigns**
Brand films, explainers, motion graphics and AI-assisted animation. Social media, ads and
campaign design.

**Public sector and institutions**
Municipal and institutional portals, intranets, citizen service platforms, e-learning and
accessibility compliance.

**Web3**
dApps, smart contract interfaces, wallet integrations, token launch sites, asset
tokenization and on-chain dashboards.

**Consulting**
Technical architecture, SEO, performance and accessibility audits, and CTO as a service.

**Hosting and ongoing work**
Managed hosting, monitoring and backups, continuous development after launch, and monthly
editorial content.
